# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Two-deck A/B comparison with a shared playhead, level matching, time
  alignment, and blind A/B and ABX tests.
- Click or drag the waveform to seek; Shift-drag sets a loop region.
- LUFS, true-peak, loudness-range and correlation metering; monitor folds.
- Detached equaliser window with live spectrum and band audition.
- Warm-neutral default appearance, light/dark/system themes, custom accents,
  and an importable theme document; reduced-motion support.
- Universal macOS build (11.3+) and Windows x64 installer; releases are
  published automatically from `main`.
